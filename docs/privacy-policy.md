# Ranse — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 August 2026
**Publisher:** Data Druid Tech Services Limited, Abuja, Nigeria

Ranse is an email client. It connects your phone directly to the mail server
that hosts your email account. We built it so that your mail and your
credentials stay between your phone and your mail server.

## What Ranse stores on your phone

- Your email address, display name, mail-server settings, and password.
  The password is stored in Android's encrypted Keystore-backed storage and
  never leaves your phone except to sign in to your own mail server.
- Copies of messages you open, cached temporarily for display.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Ranse has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no
server of ours in the mail path. Your messages travel directly between your
phone and your email provider's server over encrypted connections
(SSL/STARTTLS). We cannot see your email address, your password, or your
mail.

## Network connections Ranse makes

- Your mail server (IMAP and SMTP) — to read and send your email.
- GitHub's public API (sideloaded builds only) — to check whether a newer
  version of the app exists. This request carries no personal data. Builds
  installed from Google Play do not make this request; Play delivers updates.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to tell you when new mail arrives.
- **Internet** — to reach your mail server.

## Data deletion

Removing an account in Ranse, or uninstalling the app, deletes all stored
credentials and cached mail from the phone. Mail on your server is never
touched.
